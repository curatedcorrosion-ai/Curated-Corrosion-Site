OCI development archive

The Laboratory

The record begins while the questions are still open.

Each OCI keeps one continuing history as it moves through development. These progress labels describe the instrument’s current stage; dated observations and earlier revisions remain preserved.

01

On the breadboard

Circuit exploration, Formulas, initial measurements, and working questions.

02

Test lab

Bench evaluation, bias, stability, noise, output, and documented musical comparisons.

03

OCI in production

The chosen revision becomes a physical instrument, with construction decisions and linked specimens recorded.

04

OCI Finalized

The completed instrument’s record gains an individual link within its assigned collection. Development history remains available.

OCI entries forthcoming

Each entry will carry its OCI identity, Family, Formulas, collection, current label, dated revisions, schematic references, linked OCS specimens, testing, listening context, and photographs. Service and replacement history continue after finalization.

The Laboratory workspace tracks development across all four collections. The Laboratory Collection is the home for instruments whose collection purpose is experimentation.

Formula → Family → Individual Instrument

A Formula is a developed stage or recipe. A Family is a design lineage. An OCI is an individual instrument with its own evidence and history. Two instruments in one Family may have distinct voices; each deserves an individual record.
