Curated
Corrosion

Every specimen has a story to tell.

Curated Corrosion studies, documents, and preserves electronic component specimens before carefully returning selected examples to useful life through experimental audio circuits and handcrafted musical instruments.

Every component deserves the respect of being understood.

The Workshop begins with curiosity: identify the specimen, preserve its provenance, characterize its behavior, document what can be supported by evidence, and learn what happens when it returns to a circuit.

The Instruments
